## Description

Tired of stopping between two half-visible Reddit posts and nudging the page to see one properly? Post-by-Post snaps your feed so every post lands exactly at the top of the screen, just below Reddit's header. One flick, one post.

Try it for 15 minutes and you won't go back.

WORKS THE WAY YOU ALREADY SCROLL
• Mouse wheel and trackpad: one flick moves exactly one post (trackpad momentum is absorbed, so you never skip past posts)
• Keyboard: J / ↓ for the next post, K / ↑ for the previous one
• Space and Page Down / Page Up move one post at a time too
• Scrollbar: click or drag it and the page settles on the nearest post; the ▲/▼ arrows still make small adjustments

SMART, NOT PUSHY
• Long posts scroll normally until you reach their end, then snap
• Typing in a comment or search box is never interrupted
• Menus, pop-ups and zoom work as usual
• Reddit keeps loading more posts as you go
• Works on Home, Popular, subreddits and Search; leaves post pages alone

PRO ($3.99, one-time purchase)
• Auto-scroll: hands-free browsing, one post every 3–120 seconds. Space pauses and resumes it, with a YouTube-style pause indicator
• Custom hotkeys: pick your own keys for next and previous
• On/off switch: turn snapping off for a while without uninstalling

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Post-by-Post collects nothing. No tracking, no analytics, no accounts. Your settings stay in your browser.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Reddit, Inc.
