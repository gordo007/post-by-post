Scroll Reddit one post at a time.

No more stopping between two half-visible posts. Post-by-Post snaps your feed so every scroll or key press lands the next post neatly at the top of the screen.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store

"Try it for 15 minutes and you won't go back."

Works the way you already scroll

Mouse and trackpad

One flick moves exactly one post. Trackpad momentum is absorbed, so you never fly past posts.

Keyboard

J / ↓ next post, K / ↑ previous. Space and Page Down work too.

Scrollbar

Click or drag it and the page settles on the nearest post. The ▲/▼ arrows still make small adjustments.

Smart, not pushy

Long posts scroll normally until their end. Typing, menus and zoom are never interrupted.

Everywhere you browse

Home, Popular, subreddits and Search. Post pages are left alone.

Private by design

Collects nothing. No tracking, no analytics, no accounts. Settings stay in your browser.
