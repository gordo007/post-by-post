Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Post-by-Post is built to need as little as possible. This policy covers the Post-by-Post browser extension and this website, provided by Kramerica Industries.

The extension collects nothing

The extension does not collect, store, send, sell or share any personal information, browsing history, Reddit content or usage data. It contains no analytics or tracking.

Settings

Your settings (snapping on/off, auto-scroll timer, hotkeys) are saved with your browser's built-in extension storage. If you use browser sync, your browser may sync them between your own devices. They are never sent to us.

Where it runs

The extension runs only on reddit.com, where it adjusts scrolling. It reads the page only to find where posts begin and end.

Purchases

When you buy Pro, the payment is handled by our reseller Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record, under Paddle's privacy policy. We never see your card details.

To unlock Pro, you enter your purchase email in the extension. It is sent to postbypost.app, which asks Paddle whether that email has a Pro purchase, and answers yes or no. We don't store it on our servers. The extension keeps it in your browser's extension storage and re-checks it about once a week, so refunds are reflected. We never use it for marketing, and never sell or share it.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing support@postbypost.app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
